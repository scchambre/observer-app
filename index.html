Your position
Teams swap ends every point but you don't. Your clock is 80s (pull) when the pulling team is at your end, 60s (readiness) when the receiving team is. The app tracks which end the pull comes from after each goal — “Role looks wrong” flips it if the on-field reality differs.
Auxiliary clocks (timeout / halftime / disc-in)
These run in the same clock display. Halftime gives 5-min & 2-min calls. (Use the main Timeout button for the offence/defence setup clock.)
